Problem · Dikkat kya hai

FormSaathi is for people who find forms hard — people who fear forms, read less, or are elderly. The details a form asks for are already on their own papers, but copying them into the right boxes is the hard part.

Solution · Hal kya hai

Give photos of your documents. FormSaathi reads what it can, asks only for the few things it could not find, and shows you the full filled form to check. It then stops. Nothing moves ahead until you press OK.

Outcome · Nateeja kya milta hai

You get the filled form, ready to print or save as a PDF. You stay in control at the last step — the form is only marked ready after your OK. This page is a demo, so nothing is really sent anywhere.
